# Illustrative Information Governance Framework

This document is a portfolio artifact, not an FNIGC policy.

## Governance checkpoints
1. Purpose and authority
2. Rights-holder / partner and cultural considerations
3. Information classification and sensitivity
4. Stewardship and accountability
5. Access and approved use
6. Storage / possession / residency considerations
7. Retention and disposition
8. Security and privacy controls
9. Auditability and incident response
10. Review, measurement and continuous improvement

## Decision record
Every material information-system decision should capture:
- decision owner and approving authority;
- affected information domains;
- stakeholders / partners consulted;
- risks and mitigations;
- access and retention rules;
- implementation owner and review date;
- evidence required for closure.
