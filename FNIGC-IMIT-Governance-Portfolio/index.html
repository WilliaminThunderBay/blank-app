Stewardship & accessDefine custodianship, least-privilege access, retention, classification, and approved uses.
04
Review & accountabilityRecord approvals, monitor use, measure impact, audit access, and revisit decisions as context changes.

OCAP®-informed planning prompts

These prompts do not claim formal OCAP® compliance; they show how governance conversations can be made explicit.

Ownership Who collectively holds rights and responsibilities relating to the information?
Control Who participates in decisions about collection, use, disclosure and governance?
Access What access is appropriate, by whom, under what conditions, and how is it recorded?
Possession Where is information physically/logically held, and how does custody support governance authority?
